# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all contributors, maintainers, and participants to treat each other with respect and kindness.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful** — Treat everyone with courtesy and dignity
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome people of all backgrounds and perspectives
- **Be constructive** — Provide helpful feedback and solutions
- **Be professional** — Keep discussions focused and on-topic
- **Be honest** — Give honest feedback and acknowledge mistakes

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following are NOT tolerated in any community space:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Discrimination based on identity or characteristics
- Sexual or violent language or imagery
- Spam, trolling, or bad-faith arguments
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Threats or intimidation
- Off-topic political or religious arguments
- Any other behavior that makes others uncomfortable

## Reporting Issues

### If You Experience or Witness Inappropriate Behavior

1. **Do not engage** with the person or behavior
2. **Contact maintainers privately** (see README for contact info)
3. **Provide details**:
   - What happened?
   - When and where?
   - Any witnesses or evidence?
   - How did it affect you?

### Our Response

We will:
- Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
- Investigate fairly and confidentially
- Take appropriate action
- Respect everyone's privacy

This may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal depending on severity.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub issues and discussions
- Pull request comments
- Discord server (if applicable)
- Any official project spaces
- Offline spaces representing the project

## Diversity & Inclusion

We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for everyone. This includes:

- People of all genders, gender identities, and sexual orientations
- People of all races, ethnicities, nationalities, and skin colors
- People from all backgrounds and socioeconomic statuses
- People of all abilities and disabilities
- People from all ages
- People with all religious and non-religious beliefs

## Leadership Responsibility

Community leaders (maintainers) will:
- Lead by example
- Address violations promptly and fairly
- Maintain confidentiality when appropriate
- Recuse themselves if directly involved
- Work to resolve conflicts constructively
